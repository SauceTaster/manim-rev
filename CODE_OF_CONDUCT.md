# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Manim Rev is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. We
value people of every background, identity, ability, experience level, and
viewpoint who participate in good faith.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- discuss ideas and work without attacking a person's character;
- communicate with patience, empathy, and technical honesty;
- give and receive specific, constructive feedback;
- credit sources and other contributors;
- respect stated boundaries, privacy, and confidentiality;
- take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, impersonation, sustained disruption, and retaliation
against a reporter are not permitted. Publishing another person's private
information without explicit permission is also prohibited.

Security and reverse-engineering discussions require extra care. Do not use
the project community to target people, expose live credentials, coordinate
unauthorized access, or share harmful material without the context and
safeguards needed for legitimate research or education.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review threads,
community events, and other spaces managed by the project. It also applies
when someone officially represents Manim Rev elsewhere.

## Reporting

Do not place incident details, identities, screenshots, or private contact
information in a public issue. Use the repository's **Private conduct
contact** issue form only to ask a maintainer to arrange a confidential
channel. A maintainer will follow up privately. If contacting the project is
unsafe, use GitHub's platform reporting tools instead.

Maintainers will protect reporter confidentiality as far as practical,
consider conflicts of interest, and investigate reports promptly and fairly.
Good-faith reports will not be punished, even when an investigation does not
confirm a violation.

## Enforcement

Responses will be proportional to impact and may include clarification, a
private warning, limits on participation, temporary suspension, or permanent
removal from project spaces. Serious incidents may skip intermediate steps.
Maintainers may remove content that violates this policy and will avoid
publicly sharing private enforcement details unless safety requires it.
